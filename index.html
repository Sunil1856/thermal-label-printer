🌐 Connect & Print from Other Computers

ThermalPrint CAD is running live on your local network (LAN / Wi-Fi). You can open it and print from any other desktop, laptop, or tablet connected to the same Wi-Fi router!

1

Open on any browser

On your second desktop or laptop, open Chrome or Edge and paste the link above.

2

Print directly

Hit Print! The label will print to your thermal printer without needing any installation on the other PC.
